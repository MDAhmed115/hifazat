# Data Handling Policy (MOCK for hackathon)

The department stores only the data needed to process cases: name, phone number, CNIC number, the messages a citizen submits, and the decisions made about them. Data is kept for 12 months and is used only for relief services and fraud prevention. It is never sold or shared with private companies.

Citizens have the right to see the data stored about them, to ask for a correction, and to withdraw consent. Every automatic decision has an explanation and an audit record, and citizens can ask for a human officer to review any decision.
