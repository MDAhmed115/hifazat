# Official Complaint Procedure for Scam Messages (MOCK for hackathon)

To report a scam SMS, take a screenshot of the message or copy its full text. Do not reply to it and do not click any link in it. Submit the message through the Hifazat portal, or call the mock helpline 0800-00000 (MOCK), and mention the sender number.

An officer reviews every reported message within 3 working days and may block the reported phone number or account. Complaints about unpaid relief payments follow the same route: give your CNIC's last 4 digits and the month when the payment was missed.
