# Announcement: No Advance Fees for Relief Payments (MOCK for hackathon)

Official announcement: relief payments NEVER require an advance fee. Any message that asks you to send money first, to pay bank charges, a processing fee, a tax or a delivery fee in order to receive relief is fraud.

Do not share your OTP, PIN or card details with anyone claiming to be from the relief department. Prize and lucky-draw messages are not sent by the department. Report such messages immediately using the official complaint procedure.
