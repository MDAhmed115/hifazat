# Kafaalat-Style Widow Support Rules (MOCK for hackathon)

Eligibility: widows registered in the national database with a monthly household income below PKR 30,000 can apply for quarterly widow support. The application is free and can be submitted at any district relief office.

Payments are sent quarterly through Raast to the widow's own account. Officials never ask for a commission, a fee or a share of the payment. Any person asking for money to approve or speed up a payment should be reported using the official complaint procedure.
