# Bait-ul-Maal Relief Scheme Rules (MOCK for hackathon)

Eligibility: a household qualifies for the Bait-ul-Maal Relief scheme if its total monthly income is below PKR 40,000. Applying is completely free. No agent, shop or website may charge any fee for an application.

Payments are made ONLY through Raast to the beneficiary's own verified account. The government NEVER asks for cash, bank charges, processing fees or advance payments to release a relief payment. Nobody from the department will ask for your OTP or PIN.
